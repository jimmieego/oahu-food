# Oahu food shortlist from Four Seasons Ko Olina

**Starting point:** Four Seasons Resort Oahu at Ko Olina, 92-1001 Olani Street, Kapolei, HI 96707.

**Starting point coordinates:** 21.33930, -158.12574

Travel times below are approximate, one-way planning estimates from the hotel. Driving ranges are rounded from Google Maps routes and include some traffic allowance, but exclude parking and walking from the car. H-1 rush-hour traffic can take longer.

Places with a post recommendation come from another traveler's Oahu trip report. In the notes, "the poster" is that traveler. Each place keeps what the poster said apart from the restaurant's own menu highlights. **Bogart's fried rice is the only dish that the poster named.**

Places with a commenter recommendation come from replies to two Oahu trip-planning threads. The note shows how many commenters recommended the place. Travel times for these places come from OpenStreetMap routes and do not include traffic. Hours change often, so check before you go.
