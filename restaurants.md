# Oahu food shortlist from Four Seasons Ko Olina

**Starting point:** Four Seasons Resort Oahu at Ko Olina, 92-1001 Olani Street, Kapolei, HI 96707.

**Starting point coordinates:** 21.33930, -158.12574

Travel times below are approximate, one-way planning estimates from the hotel. Driving ranges are rounded from Google Maps routes and include some traffic allowance, but exclude parking and walking from the car. H-1 rush-hour traffic can take longer.

The recommendations come from another traveler's Oahu trip report. In the notes, "the poster" is that traveler. Each place keeps what the poster said apart from the restaurant's own menu highlights. **Bogart's fried rice is the only dish that the poster named.**

## Nearby in Ko Olina

### 1. Monkeypod Kitchen Ko Olina

- **Address:** 92-1048 Olani Street, Kapolei, HI 96707.
- **Coordinates:** 21.34159, -158.12347
- **Approximate travel time:** 5–10 minutes walking, or 2–5 minutes driving. The mapped walk is about 0.3 mile and 5 minutes.
- **Food to consider:** Wood-fired pizza, highlighted on the restaurant’s official location page.
- **Post recommendation:** The poster loved Monkeypod but did not name a dish. They visited the **Waikiki branch**; this is the nearby Ko Olina branch.
- **Sources:** [Official restaurant page](https://www.monkeypodkitchen.com/dine_ko_olina) · [Walking directions from Four Seasons](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Four+Seasons+Resort+Oahu+at+Ko+Olina&destination=Monkeypod+Kitchen+Ko+Olina&travelmode=walking).

### 2. Roy’s Ko Olina

- **Address:** 92-1220 Aliinui Drive, Kapolei, HI 96707, at Ko Olina Golf Club.
- **Coordinates:** 21.34365, -158.12439
- **Approximate travel time:** 3–5 minutes driving.
- **Food to consider:** Misoyaki butterfish and braised short rib, featured together on the restaurant’s official prix fixe menu.
- **Connection to the post:** A nearby Roy Yamaguchi alternative. The poster recommended **Beach House by Roy Yamaguchi on the North Shore**, a different restaurant, and did not name a dish.
- **Sources:** [Official restaurant page](https://www.royyamaguchi.com/roys-ko-olina) · [Driving directions from Four Seasons](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Four+Seasons+Resort+Oahu+at+Ko+Olina&destination=Roys+Ko+Olina+92-1220+Aliinui+Dr&travelmode=driving).

## Aiea and Honolulu / Waikiki

### 3. Ryder’s Poke and Shave Ice — Aiea

- **Address:** 99-016 Kamehameha Highway, First Floor, Aiea, HI 96701, near Dixie Grill.
- **Coordinates:** 21.37829, -157.93516
- **Approximate travel time:** 25–40 minutes driving. The route showed about 22 minutes and 17 miles when checked.
- **Food to consider:** Poke bowls and shave ice; no particular poke flavor or shave-ice combination was named in the post.
- **Post recommendation:** One of the poster’s strongest recommendations; they praised the food and friendly, helpful service.
- **Location clarification:** The post labels this “Honolulu,” but the verified location is **Aiea**, closer to Ko Olina than Waikiki.
- **Sources:** [Official restaurant website](https://ryspokexshaveice.wixsite.com/home) · [Driving directions from Four Seasons](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Four+Seasons+Resort+Oahu+at+Ko+Olina&destination=Ryders+Poke+and+Shave+Ice+99-016+Kamehameha+Hwy+Aiea&travelmode=driving).

- **Address:** 2255 Kalakaua Avenue, Honolulu, HI 96815, inside Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort.
